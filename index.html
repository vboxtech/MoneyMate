Privacy Policy for MoneyMate

Last Updated: June 2026

Introduction

MoneyMate is a personal finance and expense tracking application designed to help users manage their income and expenses, view financial analytics, generate charts, and export monthly PDF statements.

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyMate handles data when you use the application.

Information We Collect

MoneyMate does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require:

  • User registration
  • Account creation
  • Login or signup
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location information
  • Contact information

Financial Data

The financial information you enter into MoneyMate, including income records, expense records, transaction dates, categories, amounts, and notes, is stored locally on your device only.

This data is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible by us.

Internet Usage

MoneyMate can function completely offline. An internet connection is only used to display advertising banners when available.

Advertising

MoneyMate may display banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect limited device-related information in accordance with its own privacy practices to provide and improve advertising services.

Learn more about Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties. Since financial data remains on the user's device, we do not have access to it.

Data Security

All financial records are stored locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for securing access to their device using passwords, PINs, biometrics, or other security measures.

PDF Export

MoneyMate allows users to generate and download PDF statements based on their own financial records. PDF files are generated locally on the device.

Children's Privacy

MoneyMate is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
